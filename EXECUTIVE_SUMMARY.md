## Unified Mechanistic Narrative

**Lactate is the central node** linking all therapeutic strategies:

1. **GALR3 blockade** depletes lactate-producing MDSCs/TAMs
2. **Ferroptosis induction** overcomes lactate-mediated SLC7A11/GPX4 resistance
3. **Checkpoint blockade** unmasks T-cell response after immune infiltration
4. **RAS(ON) inhibition** blocks proliferation *after* immune pressure established
5. **CDK4/6 inhibition** creates metabolic bottleneck in surviving tumor cells
6. **VEGF-A blockade** (HARMONi-6, Arm 9) reduces hypoxia → lowers lactate production

**Result:** Ecosystem-centric precision oncology that addresses tumor cell + immune cell + stromal cell + metabolic node simultaneously.
